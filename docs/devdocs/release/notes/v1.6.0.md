### Added
- **A choice for diagrams that cannot share a page**: when a diagram or image will not fit in the space left on the page, the PDF export prints it whole on the next page or splits it across the break — `prettyMarkdown.oversizedDiagrams` settles the choice once.
- **Themes and per-component colours**: pick `default`, `github`, `dark`, or `sepia` with `prettyMarkdown.theme`, and recolour any component through `prettyMarkdown.colors` or the colour pickers in the settings page. The preview, exported PDFs, and the web build share one palette, and the preview updates the moment a colour changes.

### Changed
- **Wider text in exported PDFs**: the side margins are halved, from 15 mm to 7.5 mm.

### Fixed
- **Page-sized gaps in exported PDFs**: a tall diagram, image, or code block no longer strands a near-empty page. Diagrams and images are scaled to fit alongside the heading that prints with them, code blocks break across pages, and anything too tall to shrink and stay readable is printed across the break.
- **Mermaid error graphic could still reach a PDF**: a diagram block is restored to its source text immediately before the page is printed, so mermaid's "Syntax error" image never lands in the export.
